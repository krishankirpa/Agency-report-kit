FAQ · AGENCY REPORT KIT

Questions marketers ask before they switch.

See how Agency Report Kit turns messy campaign exports into faster decisions, cleaner client communication and a repeatable reporting workflow.

Agency Report Kit turns campaign CSV exports into a structured performance report with KPIs, campaign analysis, insights, scale signals, next actions and client-ready sharing. It is built to replace repetitive spreadsheet-to-slide reporting work.
The product helps you spend less time formatting reports and more time on optimization, strategy and client conversations. A repeatable reporting flow makes it easier to handle more accounts without rebuilding every report from scratch.
The report engine is designed for major performance-marketing exports such as Meta Ads and Google Ads, with a flexible Custom/Other CSV path for compatible data from additional platforms.
Yes. After signing in, you can add client accounts, select a client while building a report, save reports against that client and keep client-level report history in your account.
Depending on the source export, the report can analyze spend, results or conversions, CPL/CPA, impressions, clicks, CTR, revenue, ROAS, funnel steps, campaign performance and period comparisons.
The product does not invent revenue or ROAS. When the source data does not contain a valid revenue or platform-reported ROAS field, ROAS is shown as unavailable and the report focuses on the metrics that are actually present.
Yes. Branding lets you add your company or agency name, tagline and logo. That identity can be reflected in the report preview and client-ready exported reports.
Yes. You can choose from the available report templates and preview the current data in the selected design before downloading or sharing the report.
You can download the selected report as a PDF, use the Share Report/Share PDF workflow where supported, and open a pre-filled Gmail message for a fast client handoff.
Yes. The workflow is designed for agencies, freelancers, creators and growth teams that need a faster way to turn campaign data into a professional client deliverable without building every report manually.
